When you rent a car, Budget places a pre-authorisation hold on your credit card. This is not a charge but a temporary hold to cover the estimated rental charges and any additional costs (fuel, extra hours, etc). The unused amount will be released after the rental ends.

The amount of the pre-authorisation or security deposit is either fixed - or calculated based on:

  • The estimated vehicle rental price.
  • Less any amount we accept you have paid towards the rental when you booked - for example, the cost of any accepted voucher.
  • Plus, the excess fees based on the vehicles group.
